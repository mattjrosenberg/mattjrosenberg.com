[Ugly Duckling Union](https://open.spotify.com/album/6g8JRKz0Wx6shPLZoCFgLb?si=FjPWTMaWQnu1Ib_PYPAgZQ) - [[Lowertown]]

This is so emotionally exposed across the whole album. It’s like almost song was written as if being delivered with anguish over the phone to a lost lover. This is all high praise from me - this really dug into me. Creative and expert composition while the perfect writing to drive the songs into being memorable. It’s a bit experimental and they move around genres to mostly success.

→Check out 'Echo of Desire'

[Resistance](https://open.spotify.com/album/7tSXgQdkbNATcCYbOzIjW4?si=qGpN_8ZER7qABSCPfVVpWw) - [[CQ Wrestling]]

Sitting on an Amtrak to Boston, popped the AirPods in first thing and put this. Instantly lower my heart rate. I’ve never listened to this band before but instantly felt so familiar but not regular. It was tough to pick a song - they truly are all good. The best kind of UK guitar music.

→Check out 'HEALTH+'

[Logbook](https://open.spotify.com/album/50MAcCKr4n9eOA2jPgPZm2?si=Fi02CRjBQvCoI5dPTh_-jQ) - [[Crash of Rhinos]]

You could almost feel the rust be shaken off as this album goes on - their first in over a decade. At 7 songs, 38 minutes this isn’t a long album but they’re longer songs. Usually not my preference for this genre, but they make the most of the canvas to build their sound. And their sound is truly great.

→Check out 'Errata'

[Birds of Paradise Dear Life](https://open.spotify.com/album/7Lib6qYBmpQCGyh1W8L4S3?si=JNgedykrSQm35tCe80c8Fg) - [[Thomas Dollbaum]]

So upsetting to hear this has been sitting on a shelf for a few years because of label nonsense. It’s an amazing album. Upbeat, raw, and true country indie while being soft and tender when it needs to be. Excellent writing. Astonishing momentum. And just great guitars the whole way through.

→Check out 'Coyote'
