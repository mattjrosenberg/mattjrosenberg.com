[WIRED](https://open.spotify.com/album/2ZOCPqWk1v5UXSBHqOC151?si=50dxJQoBSwK3tcQRVTorSQ) - [[Basement]]

This is how you make an album. Loved where this album started and brought. Headbanging guitars and fantastic vocals. As expected, it is just incredibly well written. The song recommended below into the next, 'Embrace', shows the width of this effort. Fast, hard, loud  into a melodic dreamscape that lulls you into thinking it's going to be a quiet time. It's not going to be. There's a reason Basement is one of the (if not the) leaders in the clubhouse when it comes to modern hardcore. Loved this album.

→Check out 'Pick up the pieces'

[Cost of Living Adjustment](https://open.spotify.com/album/33qjdHHhpYlh1GcXoI5knH?si=1YNTmR3IQX-CUeM0gnZ0Ng) - [[Cola]]

I love the lead singer's voice. It has such a depth and groove with being the perfect level of just - odd? This is a groovy album with great punk roots. Pretty great for a band named after an economic acronym. Loved this. 

→Check out 'Haveluck Country'

[Barely Here](https://open.spotify.com/album/7wRLUzbIKo1teiHHukMg2U?si=bX11Nmp7Rd-WpNQGEXDCdA) - [[Koyo]]

This took exactly 4 seconds to get started and just put a smile on my face at the first song. It's fast, it's loud, and it is so well written. This album really just rips your face off with guitars and noise without ever losing you as a listener. Expert work. 

→Check out 'Saying Vs. Meaning'
