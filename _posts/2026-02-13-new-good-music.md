[Masquerade](https://open.spotify.com/album/29AoeHJhdqdvkZAh7EPoS4?si=9yivMWb3RhynnY79i3pVRA) - Cardinals
	This is incredible. Few bands have had me hooked within the first few seconds. A sound that pays so much homage to both where they are from and the lineage of music they're making (both Celtic and indie rock).
	→Check out 'Over At Last'

[Cold 2 the Touch](https://open.spotify.com/album/2FPvnCC5H66sUAnNSgQ7hK?si=220tQVyQQ92DZWJfASCjSw) - Angel Du$t
	Fast, melodic and loud punk/hardcore music that'll be at home with any Turnstile fan (even if they pre-date that particular band). 
	→Check out 'I'm The Outside.'

[Clearly Cursed](https://open.spotify.com/album/6SJ2ZlM0zlgbJr6bFYAAjx?si=-Pgv0eLvQCmwQR6qqivMpA) - PONY
	Love just the sound of this. It feels full, it's expertly crafted and mixes pop with rock so well. 
	→Check out 'Hot and Mean'

[The Refrigerator](https://open.spotify.com/album/6BJcPDec1HjMQ0H3WyWckR?si=CwDj-baEQQGuuX_CRHt4cw) - Remember Sports
	I don't know where 'Tenderpunk' came as a genre but it is spot on for this. Lovely writing and composition for this indie album
	→Check out 'Wowie'

[Love is Not Enough ](https://open.spotify.com/album/1ZiHuZUECf9saUhmv8vYxm?si=EKINPzBhT4ysPfV-fYpRBg)- Converge
	Another album this week from absolute legends but this one actually rules. Just the exact type of metal I like - fast, loud, and a drum-forward.
	→Check out 'Bad Faith'
