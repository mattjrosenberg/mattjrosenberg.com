[Philadelphia's been good to me](https://open.spotify.com/album/5AfELtBFbN1hM75hgqc7FI?si=daTrcrEJSZuhVmvNXwWk3Q) - [[Kurt Vile]]

It's Kurt Vile, man. It's good. It's incredibly well written. It should be played through the speakers of a beat up truck. He's a 76er's fan (presumably) so no wonder it's a bit melancholy.

→Check out '99th Song'

[For Love of Grace & the Hereafter](https://open.spotify.com/album/1oU9h86D6qcuHNDoGvzpwV?si=ztQZ7Z8HREKfKvif8B-x7Q) - [[Iceage]]

I get that they are called punk. And they are. But this is pure indie rock. It has a vibe and sensibility that is catchy. Bounding drums are surround be excellent, and shrewd, guitar work that let's the vocals play the leading role they should.

→Check out 'Lifetime'

[Beauty Land](https://open.spotify.com/album/60obFnzuMGgVR5bHKioyX2?si=Ep1OQVTKQx-yzv8NdL_PcA) - [[Greg Mendez]]

Really loved this. It's so touching and beautifully constructed in its simplicity. They're short songs and it moves quickly through them but there's no much else to say but it's a charming listen that I recommended.

→Check out 'So Mean'

[Your God Fearing Days Are About To Begin](https://open.spotify.com/album/7wEu9yFSpjOSLVYY9EAaza?si=SP7z-8XHQXSDyIw-sf3j3Q) - [[Saint Agnes]]

I have such a soft spot for this type of theatrical, sort of punk and sort of metal and sort of hard rock, music. This is expertly done. The first song, 'Good Boy,' is funny and just feels like something that could only come from this artist. It hits the fast and the slow so well.

→Check out 'The Beast'

[Blurred Away](https://open.spotify.com/album/70hdn5Yv2a0p3pGyoNBQWc?si=V9LLfnGqQvG0L47SbycuxA) - [[Oakwood]]

Emo is undefeated. One song just opens with 'what is the point of life?' This rules. I wish it was twice as long. I could live in this screaming for so long. The guitars and drums are groovy, carrying a great rhythm through this whole effort. Just a wonderful album.

→Check out 'Serpentine (My Whole Life)'

[Bitknot](https://open.spotify.com/album/5wpfyoOxAVSVtzszRHVcIZ?si=NeUoTy2_T2Cp1PwJ0xzBcw) - [[Feeble Little Horse]]

What a cool album. Really takes a best of a bunch of genres to mix it up. It's certainly more shoegaze with some electric, but it had a folk sensibility that really keyed me in on the morning I listened. It's full blown summer now in NYC, and I just want this on while I lay in the park.

→Check out 'Upside Down'
