[Hoopla](https://open.spotify.com/album/4OmO1a6jKotWwguSeQzLBa?si=MXYSUv3MSSGR85yyHruOFg) - [[Weird Nightmare]]

This is the find of today's releases which were weaker than usual but that doesn't take from what is a fun album. An album where I just had a hard time deciding what song to call out because each one kept grabbing attention. It's fun, feels almost like [[Incubus]] in its composition and song structure while being pure indie rock. 

→Check out 'Little Strange'

[I’m People](https://open.spotify.com/album/7JFzMd0mR1HqVjuXKN5M7u?si=35hHa1swTdi2sMuoxFqnVg) - [[Hiss Golden Messenger]]

What a lovely album. His voice is almost [[Bob Dylan]]-esque? Found myself floating through this album as the writing and excellent musicality of it just worked really great. The best of what modern folk can be even if there's nothing really modern about it. 

→Check out 'Mercy Avenue'

[Middle of Nowhere](https://open.spotify.com/album/1quFS6EneVBW7PTbipPlNg?si=nYj81FnrQpyH03F7qN5N1g) - [[Kacey Musgraves]]

It's a Kacey album! This doesn't feel that different from her last full length effort but that's a good thing. It's a grounded, well-written and story-forward album that uses her celebrity to bring out some big names to feature alongside her. I find her music to be expert that lovely soundtracking to a lot of a day's activities to being the forefront of attention. Her latest phase as an excellent shelf life. And for the first time, I'm recommending the big-hit single because I enjoyed it so much. 

→Check out 'Dry Spell'

[Exuvia](https://open.spotify.com/album/03lLTw4BTb1J6sEl8s1bc3?si=j5eiskHoSnq7sYsgRdy7eA) - [[Cage Fight]]

I am not sure the last thing I listened to that so deeply reminded me of Five Finger Death Punch - one of my favorite bands when I was younger. This goes hard and does give the listener any space to let up. That being said, I quite liked when they brought in some rock vocals over the driving screaming that is purposive through the whole album. Just a head banging masterclass. 

→Check out 'Un Bon Souvenir'
