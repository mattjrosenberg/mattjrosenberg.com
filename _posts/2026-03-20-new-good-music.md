[No Need to Be Loney](https://open.spotify.com/album/1mficSVPDsJaBQoa5k9fTk?si=JGPFrqmzTCSwEOytxKpAFQ) - [[Gladie]]

I love how fuzzy this sounds. Like real, old-school guitar distortion. The vocals match - raw at times, loud at times and very emotive. The song suggested below (Fix Her) is really a journey and one that felt great to go down - even if the song isn't the happiest. 

→Check out 'Fix Her'

[Razor Tongue](https://open.spotify.com/album/6vCLoclFlokJbn55Y866Xq?si=ZR-PxAcZQEa4W8xhaE93pQ) - [[VERO]]

There's something so old school feeling about this - like the best of 2000s music. It's a bit [[grunge]], it's absolutely [[indie]] but it's got the best of radio rock (this is a huge compliment). But it feels new and modern. 

→Check out 'Calico'

[Brink](https://open.spotify.com/album/6YWyh2uDVJspFCq32tVPuh?si=zw1fsDQMTZW9HsyNrcJxJg) - [[Girl Scout]]

Loved this - so thoughtful for a first full-length album. Vocals and writing are inviting and full of feeling. The composition is spot on for what this type of indie should be.  

→Check out 'Uh-Huh'

[Butterfly](https://open.spotify.com/album/3BB0xOQPvpn5Amjh3TFBfI?si=hDljTSEzRmWWLBVwnINBTg) - [[Witch Post ]]

It's really a bummer this is only 7 songs - I could have gone at least twice as long on them. Their voices work so well together. Just listen! It's good.

→Check out 'Witching Hour' 

[Weight of the Truth](https://open.spotify.com/album/7gOX07sQgqrfaNyGJYTUoO?si=eUILBUVwTYeHoVDWynXeHQ) - [[Footballhead]]

Emo is so back and this is just another shining example. It's just a shame I listened to this at my desk and not driving a hand-me-down car from my grandparents through a suburb. 

→Check out 'Weight of the Truth'

[All Dressed Up](https://open.spotify.com/album/2J2A8C4WvbTFyZUYX1cuSr?si=JBmwZgBfStK6UwS5x50d-w) - [[Leah Blevins]]

Because of my lack of knowledge of this genre, it's unfair to just compare Leah to [[Sierra Ferrell]]. But it's really hard not to. The writing is great, the music MOVES and it's a fun listen. I hope she shows up at [[Newport Folk Festival]] this summer - would love to hear this in the sun with a beer. 

→Check out 'Leave It Up to Me'

[i sure am getting sick of this bowling alley](https://open.spotify.com/album/6WbOFGgframgYc7KIiCY5K?si=FzDKKPgvSBCMnSs7R3jJSg) - [[Mclusky]]

Really good, short punk album. I really enjoyed the energy and noise within this one. Absolutely worth listening to this and going back to listen to these old-hands. 

→Check out 'that was my brain on elves'
