[The Great Divide](https://open.spotify.com/album/2fnkyn9EybagIoFJ7a13oz?si=5nPTtRLJRxiSBB1OnbEcgA) - [[Noah Kahan]]

This New England boy was never going to not recommend this album. The music industry needs people like Noah as its stars. As expected, The Great Divide is a thoughtful and deeply felt album. The single, Porch Light, really marks a transition point because the next song (rec'd below) gets into my favorite type of music from Kahan. The type that infuses his soft, acoustic sensibilities with real edge. The amount of light beer I'm going to drink to this album this summer is too high to count. 

→Check out 'Deny Deny Deny'

[Always Sun](https://open.spotify.com/album/2eQ5f01rtUzOsneaegrQXh?si=RjZpKfr2QeGeuO6HGWliHQ) - [[Misty Coast]]

I really liked how untidy this felt. The instruments feel textured and raw. She has such a dream-like voice but really knew where to punch on the vocals. Felt surreal throwing this on as I walked into a sunny, 60 degree afternoon. Floated me along as I ran my errands. Bravo. 

→Check out 'Spring'

[What’s Left Now?](https://open.spotify.com/album/06cs9heiNyqq8voYtnDnzF?si=Gj8Vy80jQd6dNMC64tw1QA) - [[Death Lens]]

Getting [[Militarie Gun]] on a song is an easy way for me to get into an album. It's also easy to see how they were able to match their sounds. And after that feature, I really enjoyed where the album settled into. Really well crafted punk with understandable, raging lyrics and musicality.  'Debt Collector' made me want to mosh at my desk. 

→Check out 'Drown'

[13](https://open.spotify.com/album/21OjLYKI3xdQOFbPvZ0x4M?si=IHK-wdzOT7GUOx4H379IKA) - [[White Denim]]

There's a lot of interesting things happening in this album. It truly is all over the place. This might be the softest recommendation I've given but for some this will be great. 

→Check out 'Crossfyre'
