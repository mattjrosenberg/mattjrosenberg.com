[The Product](https://open.spotify.com/album/4CAUAPU45Uy007KgE5JzeC?si=bmmZm-CkRjqLrudyILqwLw) - [[Captain Jazz]]

It starts slow and quieter, turns the knob up all the way and then doesn't let up from there. This is such a cool album that does such a good job blending all the genres together. The decision when to sing and when to scream and when to blend in other elements (a fake news report at a time?) is expert. This plainly rules.

→Check out '777 Seconds'

[GREY](https://open.spotify.com/album/5mK510MU59pOQFk1OLLol8?si=iog1oMIZTMeBB2TtmfMm_A) - [[Held.]]

This rips. Every album. Let me be clear: Every. Single. Album. Should start with drum stick taps, a count, and then heavy guitar riffs. This felt so old school (early 2000s) and I loved every second of it. It's headbanging, loud, and fast. The first song ('DEFENDING THE EARTH) has a vibey bass line popping through that just made me groove.

→Check out 'EMPTINESS: A SIDE EFFECT'

[Product Of New York](https://open.spotify.com/album/6lJ4TSSnGIUdxZPZMkrx0g?si=Zt_fiQyVR1Kyc9VxwdT33g) - [[Incendiary]]

I usually try to only rec net-new albums so this is bit different but this compilation of Incendiary's early work is great to listen to. So much what represents specific scenes are hard to find or lost so revisiting it is worth the time. It was hard-driving, loud, and rough - just perfect punk music. The [[Beastie Boys]] cover at the end was well done, too.

→Check out 'Cold War'

[Change of Plans](https://open.spotify.com/album/2B4mYUeondysGay1mXFm9M?si=rdvHER3OS3i13LHXdF085Q) - [[49 Winchester]]

Love these boys. It's soulful, fun, and emotional. It's never the heaviest lift to list and that's what I love about it. There's a care and technique put in the composition and writing that isn't showing. Just great stuff.

→Check out 'Pardon Me'

[It Comes in Waves](https://open.spotify.com/album/11oKN8d2sj4SQwzv2127sv?si=j3L-EOshTY2_jH0TCJ1HXw) - [[New Constellations]]

This album just made me happy. It's just a great pop album that a lot of thought and creativity went into. Put it on during the pregame.

→Check out 'Do What You Want'

[American Stories](https://open.spotify.com/album/2lAFWRd94TbhmZUCUthoso?si=eixsV--5RCWZVTHQmn_7Ow) - [[Rostam]]

An incredibly earnest effort. There is a consistent heartbeat throughout each song that really lets the whole experience of the album feel cohesiveness. I really enjoy efforts that demand they be experienced whole and not just through singles. The use Clairo on the song below is extremely well done.

→Check out 'Hardy'

[Little Wide Open](https://open.spotify.com/album/4SE1kVQva7akjFCMxFiP7U?si=d-zlTQJZQ5eYn1fY7vsWDQ) - [[Kevin Morby]]

It's a folk album from Kevin Morby! It's really good. It moves, has considered writing that is put excellently against bouncy, rhythmic guitars, and other strings.

→Check out 'I Ride Passenger'
