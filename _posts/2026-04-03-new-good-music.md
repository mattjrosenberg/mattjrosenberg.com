[Birding](https://open.spotify.com/album/4LRV1hTTosrrR7cdWyqyJo?si=XvwKtH7uQom1lYZItCnCtw) - [[deary]]

This is plainly just amazing. Every once and a while, we need a really good dreamy album and this is that. Just rock-solid production, vocalization and writing. Some of these songs hit like hail and others envelope us like fog. 

→Check out 'Blue Ribbon'

[The Secret To Good Living](https://open.spotify.com/album/1KJdhFNC3EJC2gqXF7yOTg?si=QJCG-NOKTJC1fVlVF9WClw) - Hiding Places

There's so much to like here and so much of recent female-lead indie that this reminds me of. Beautiful arrangement and vocals with sharp writing. The song recommended below has a real beautiful acoustic guitar arrangement that then threw an electric riff over and made me sit up straight. This rules. 

→Check out 'One Hand'

[Age of the Ram](https://open.spotify.com/album/7qSbOinSadHKhs2OkjKvEP?si=kKECDN4CQa6LNu4JEdl61A) - [[Charley Crockett]]

I really love Charlie Crockett - which is good, because the man produces an insane amount of music. This, his third full-length in about a year, completes the Sagebrush Trilogy. He's just a really solid musician who knows how to write very well. It's telling that it never feels boring but he doesn't confuse what he's trying to do with experimenting. 

→Check out 'Sweet Mother Texas'

[Hymns From the Hills](https://open.spotify.com/album/4dZNUFOxYuI95VGMIMZ90Q?si=7utripoVQ5ySGE1EKSsDIw) - [[Poison Ruïn]]

This got labeled as 'Dungeon synth' for reasons I refuse to look up. It rules - it's a bit [[post-punk]] (a genre that I love but in theory isn't real) as much as it's just [[hardcore]]. Very good. 

→Check out 'Hymns From the Hills'

[Glass and Bones](https://open.spotify.com/album/2TBOgMEKprOm6aJ8sKKp0O?si=Qdn5TmTdTcSGKpjRRvlZcQ) - [[Makthaverskan]]

Really catchy and poppy. I had no idea what to expect here. But it's good! Really digging the writing and vocals

→Check out 'Louie'
