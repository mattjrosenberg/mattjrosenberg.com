[IT'S THE LONG GOODBYE](https://open.spotify.com/album/2VDn7Y8xO8c09edBK3waKj?si=6jctijHwSHGG8zAe2FaWPQ) -  [[The Twilight Sad]]

This is just amazing and it's understandable why their [[Spotify]] bio just includes a glowing quote from [[The Cure]]. There's a lot of [[Robert Smith ]]in this album - from the sweeping lyrics to the pulsing rock that really underlines the whole album. It does something inexcusable at the end of the fourth song but it's worth the listen. This is going to get a lot of play from me in the foreseeable future. 

→Check out 'THE CEILING UNDERGROUND'

[Lost On You](https://open.spotify.com/album/7cK2pTXJcIC0j12IqTSip2?si=NG-NMkr2QMClnbT1LUdpEg) - [[Tigers Jaw]]

It's really amazing how much [[emo]] has had a resurgence. The newest stuff feels more mature and developed but this is another shining example of just a great album in that history of music. Great lyrics and composition. 

→Check out 'Baptized on a Redwood Drive'

[Ricochet](https://open.spotify.com/album/5b7UrNnuckYDfCp6ZW0Sji?si=FLOfL7SrQT2-YMyZfZTqow) - [[Snail Mail]]

Just lovely, well-written music. It's been too long since her last album so glad she's back with something that's so strong. The singles did well, but I'm interested to see what pops out of this. Hear her on today's [How Long Gone](https://howlonggone.com/923-snail-mail). 

→Check out 'Light On Our Feet'

[The Second I'm Asleep](https://open.spotify.com/album/38knPjMUDMSbYzHCgYlZqi?si=xJCVvIR7QKKwfBaUfiIVcQ) - [[The Boxer Rebellion]]

There's a bit of...[[The Killers]] in this? That sounds like an insult but it isn't. Great songwriting, a bit of [[pop]] and it seems like it'd be as comfortable played before a puck drop as in a loud bar. Fun album.
