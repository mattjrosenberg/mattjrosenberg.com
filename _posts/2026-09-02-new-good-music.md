Once again, emptying out the backlog. My Fridays may go back to normal soon - but who knows! 

[It Goes On](https://open.spotify.com/album/3dWKCKeiWtxjDNPTbGCQTO?si=O01JYv2QQsmRwQSmnjMMNA) - [[Westside Cowboy]]

Just really great. This got split up between the train into the city to meet a friend and then the car ride home after many stops through the fine establishments in the east side of Manhattan. It felt lively, fun, and established. Just an interesting sound and such an enjoyable listen.

→Check out 'Worried Age'

[Affirmations](https://open.spotify.com/album/6BlIVETo2sKiPSAiBmKGOr?si=mlBY3uD4TJWzpu8o1hVeqA) - [[Really Good Time]]

This album fucking rules. It's very weird and funny but god to just bangs. Loved getting into every new song, listening hard to the songwriting, and just going on the ride.

→Check out 'Bob Dylan Was On Pawn Stars in 2010'


[The Sweet Goodbye](https://open.spotify.com/album/1BX2CLG8MBglhD83tVUJvL?si=CO47flLfRmGiUb9zK-fSTQ) - [[Opus Kink]]

This, mostly because of his voice, reminded me a lot of [[Deadletter]]. This is weird. It's expressive. The composition rules. It's so well written and moody. I could have sat in this for hours and well have to when I'm not working at my desk.

→Check out 'Peckham Nocturne'

[Petal](https://open.spotify.com/album/2k4FmEtXR0WiDW0Ac2QArT?si=UezCoyCRTk64-Hg1DdA8tQ) - [[Ariana Grande]]

Trust circle, time. I love Ari. Mostly because her music, which is so involved with being around other people, was a balm during the covid years of being terrified of strangers in grocery stores. But I also appreciate she isn't subtle. She makes a raw, darker type of pop that still makes her stand out from the current crop of pop stars.

→Check out 'like i do'

[Chinook](https://open.spotify.com/album/4IYtFUfBl3Lr1yptHXNlPh?si=18Pqot75R-6jwD2U3ETUkw) - [[Mallory Hawk]]

This is moody and dark. I wish they put the vocals a bit more forward in the mix but it's effective. It's cool. It feels like watching the summer end.

→Check out 'Felicity'
