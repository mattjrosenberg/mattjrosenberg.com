> Though “Shop the Look” remains a test, it’s raising concerns from Berolzheimer and others that Instagram is using AI to take advantage of the relationships that online personalities have curated with their followers. Creator endorsements are often aligned with a particular style or image, and followers treat those endorsements like a personal stamp of approval from the media personality who is making them.

There was probably a point where we were always going to get here. That [[Instagram]] would just decide that it was easier and better for them to make money than it would be for their biggest users to make money themselves. This is a silly feature, if not a very simple one to think of, but it also will kill maybe half, if not all, of the influencers on the platform, considering the ad share can't possibly be big enough to replace any brand deals that might go away if this becomes the majority way to sell on Instagram.
