I've been slacking. While still keeping my notes on what I've been listening to, I haven't been posting. Some of that is just life. Fridays are getting more hectic, I'm traveling a bit, and also it doesn't feel right posting just 1-2 albums if that's all I get to before signing off. So here's a lot from the last few weeks that I have liked. 

[Elsewhere, Always](https://open.spotify.com/album/2TFBs5iWa52Q3AKXnCzmPj) - [[overpass]]

Pure indie British rock. The album had that brightest and lightest that is so good in this type of music. Amazing guitar structures in this. It does loud, electric and pure-rock wonderfully while being able to downshift into acoustic and soft. It feels very 2010 rock radio.

→Check out 'Get Up!'

[Easy Peeler](https://open.spotify.com/album/1I6NzZT45mQt6LSfNjavwR) - [[Brockhoff]]

This was so good. Earnest, a bit rough in the best way but just instantly got in my head and stayed there. I love her voice, the way she wrote these songs, and the way the guitars match the moment. Does a great job of adding tempo and pop to what is a true indie album.

→Check out 'Blue Star'

[Be Sweet To Me](https://open.spotify.com/album/0x4K7avY6nlNEppKxJg6fu) - [[Violet Grohl]]

Moody, gothic, and just plain good. Vacillated a lot between moods, speeds, and approaches in ways that I really quite liked.

→Check out 'Often Others'

[you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love](https://open.spotify.com/album/18qJgKH8dyYe2RRp6TbnNY) - [[Olivia Rodrigo]]

For someone who short-circuited their 'real' chance at youthful freedom, I've always appreciated how she is able to balance a deep reference set of music, inventive song construction, and real-life drenched songwriting. As one of the first [[TikTok]] breakouts, she had no real playbook. But there's a playbook to being a rockstar and a popstar. It's impressive she never hit a sophomore slump but continues to release new music that is maturing but hasn't lost the shine her very first singles did. A lot of her contemporaries can't say the same. But just a few, thinking of [[Wet Leg]] here, are. And they're creating some of the best music. Needless to say, I loved this album.

→Check out 'my way'

[New Self](https://open.spotify.com/album/29iBRlzAcmRmKRvw7onM0j) - [[The Bobby Lees]]

This was a shot of caffeine on this Friday morning. Loved this - fun, loud, and full of energy. I don't know why you'd name your band after Bobby Lee but I enjoyed this album a lot!

→Check out 'All I Got'

[A Broken Chord](https://open.spotify.com/album/6R5zu7iftwggAgbWWQu8vV) - [[Oral Habit]]

There's a lot of structured, mature, and perfect sound music out today. This isn't that and it rules. The guitars and drums have a grit, they sound like they were played in the same room and the vocals still pierce through them. A first album for these lads and it's steeped in the traditions of indie rock.

→Check out 'Thin Trippin''

[Avalanche](https://open.spotify.com/album/0on595B8sOR8yQgWnrkcmv) - [[Embrace]]

Much more beautiful than I expected based on the descriptions I read before listening. It's much more radio-rock than any of the grittier, more indy facing peers they have in the UK. Very worth the listen for just how they move throughout and using enveloping sound to force emotion.

→Check out 'Coming Home'

[The Weathervane Is My Body](https://open.spotify.com/album/2fVzzo8IRKjQOVpkcUnRQD) - [[Truck Violence]]

It's tough to know where this is going to go and the best experience is to not have expectations for it. There's so much good spread throughout this genre-bending album. I can't believe the pivot it takes in the fourth song, House caught fire, into an acoustic-folk song after some heavy screaming to open the effort. But it's all good and it's all worth it.

→Check out 'Compelled by Christy'

[Your Version](https://open.spotify.com/album/04DDuoCsQipbfl5VhQVwzD) - [[Hew]]

Absolutely rules. Right from the jump. Writing is pinpoint on t4his and backed by composing that matches. No idea what I was going to get going in but I look forward to playing this over and over.

→Check out 'How This Ends'

[Lordy](https://open.spotify.com/album/2QF10yAKT8ra06lmdKLjNX) - [[Houndmouth]]

I first saw and learned about Houndmouth in 2016, when they opened a corporate concert for [[The Killers]]. I don't remember The Killers set much because I was with my boss who was plying me with double whiskeys. But since then, Houndouth have been one of the most important bands to me. I've seen them a bunch more. Including after the 2024 election which truly soul cleansing. This feels like them growing up and adapting. I loved it. As expected, it's well written, soulful, and catchy. I'm so glad they're still making music.

→Check out 'Carbondale.'

[Florida Water Blues](https://open.spotify.com/album/2Gyxm1N8hGyBgVdApmpm4I) - [[Twisted Teens]]

I love these guys. It's so gritty and noisy but so fun, so bluesy. I appreciate a sound that is uniquely their own but is so related to so much punk coming out today.

→Check out 'Guided Thunder'

[Bring On the Psychics](https://open.spotify.com/album/12S97Ot3KogCZ3cZZoiCa0) - [[Quicksand]]

I'm going to admit something. I'm a bit hungover. It's been a stressful week, salad is suddenly bad for you and the sky is on fire. So when I put this on, the first music of the day, the punch in the face it delivered was really appreciated. It shows its teeth early but then settles into really good rock music that feels a bit Brit invasion, a bit punk, and just plainly good. Thank you for yelling at me, Quicksand.

→Check out 'Crystallize'

[Emotional Junglist](https://open.spotify.com/album/513jv6L44NNDZQGSaPNg7S) - [[Nia Archives]]

Great, great album. Fun, vibey and had a real sense of place. Just really enjoyed this.

Superlust

[You’re Gonna Need A Little Music](https://open.spotify.com/album/3OVsFANxuVt1F4Ja2Vg3bO) - [[Yard Act]]

I wish I had a more exciting word to say about this album than this but it's what I have - it's very cool. It's a bit experimental, it feels new and strange and at times no song follows the vibe set from the one before. But I really enjoyed the ride and creativity. Just listen.

→Check out 'Fiction'
