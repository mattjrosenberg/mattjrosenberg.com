Really interesting breakdown of how borrowing the design language and habits of email have ruined a whole legion of what should be pressure-free experiences online. I’m always into people looking at conventions and deciding they’re wrong. What’s even more interesting is how Godier looks at the path that we took as humans on the way to the inbox being a universal language. 

He writes a similar thing about podcasts that I think is a touch too melodramatic - I listen because they can be interesting or simply help pass the time - but the idea that our design interfaces may be too universal is interesting.
