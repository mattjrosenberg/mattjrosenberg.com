There’s a reason I try to read Matt Levine every day - at least give it a skim - because often I will learn about something I had no idea existed. The newsletter for today is one of those. Defeasance, or how bond issuers can guarantee its commitments while stripping itself of restrictions, was totally new to me. 

There’s an argument that in the case it’s being used, to eliminate the upside in the case of a change of control, should be negotiated away but bonds don’t work like that!
