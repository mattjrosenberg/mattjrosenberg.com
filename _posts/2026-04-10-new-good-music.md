[Total Dive](https://open.spotify.com/album/0mD4ce9upIZkxeSCPt7z6Q?si=jMIr684aQtipC8BUtPNxFA) - [[Brown Horse]]

Love how grumbly and rough this is. Unbelievable this is from the UK - but Europe has a weird tradition of being able to produce faithful [[Americana]] music. Thinking of one my faves, [Andrea Bignasca](https://open.spotify.com/track/6L26RxEMbGF2ckKu9Cvyxs?si=ee4aef6cc79542ff). This is so well written and performed. Need this on during a bonfire. 

→Check out 'Heat Of The Country'

[Maybe Not Tonight](https://open.spotify.com/album/7EO4tVModdOi5Xh3m7uEzC?si=91B67bWiT0S9XFKfUyuKCg) - [[Lime Garden]]

Music made for the pregame. Bouncy and fun with a tight pop core throughout the whole album. 10 songs, 29 minutes - what an effort. At times feels like a grainy 90s montage in a 2023 prestige tv show and I mean that affectionally. 

→Check out 'Cross My Heart'

[Guns Out](https://open.spotify.com/album/7BP6NegRO15kYR48tlfaX6?si=zblss4vDSl-gClWwfh4O1A) - [[Iguana Death Cult]]

There is so much of [[IDLES]] and [[Viagra Boys]] in this and it makes me so happy. The guitars have a funk, groove and edge to them that is just enjoyable to listen to. The lyrics - even the musicality - has a sense of humor that's just enjoyable. Amazing.

→Check out 'Low'

[Softest Attack](https://open.spotify.com/album/51uYxZpz3l8pJpE3fPGYb5?si=8cormeAuQoGNU2TM4tNx3A) - [[Prism Shores]]

So much to like about this really easy riding rock album. I love that they describe themselves as 'janglers' because it truly is the best word for them. You can feel each guitar strum underneath the very pop rock.

→Check out 'I Didn't Mean to Change My Mind'
