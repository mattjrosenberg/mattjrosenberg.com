[Carve](https://open.spotify.com/album/6yvVX20gPe9oQtvQZmmI8Z?si=4WLqdzgqRsu2Meba0u1n3A) - [[Kathryn Mohr]]

There's a fuzz on the guitar on the third song (Angle of Repose) that truly made my hair stand up. This is just straight up moody, gritty, and dark rock. I guess I get why it's called folk but that's not a good word for this. Grunge is. Not surprising it's mixed by the guitarist for [[Agriculture]]. 

→Check out 'I Do'

[god forbid a girl spits out her feelings!](https://open.spotify.com/album/1Ae3HjPsQvfnnZKEvOxXwN?si=DKTqDVwHRneCM2YT-MlAyQ) - [[LØLØ]]

A great pop album to kick off the first very warm Friday in NYC. Great, ground-level writing about relationships and people. Exactly what you want from this music.

→Check out 'hung up on you'

[Hotwire Trip Switch](https://open.spotify.com/album/5z68AQ3ZBayUARBTox2qk1?si=WrsJb8CoQzy5JyMsxHW5gA) - [[Prince Daddy & the Hyena]]

All I want in an emo record. Long, complicated song names. Vocals that are a little bit too nasally. Loud, rhythmic guitar riffs. Under 40 minutes. 

→Check out 'Sure Could (A Random Exercise In Life-Altering Party Fouls)'

[Superbloom](https://open.spotify.com/album/56sKKg6lpmwyvbvLw3nVCN?si=y4eHpTRQQYuLnyrZlnfFeQ) - [[Jessie Ware]]

Lovely, theatrical and fun as hell pop album. She's always worth listening to. Gets very showtunes and disco towards the end which is a fun turn. 

→Check out 'Sauna'

[Entropy](https://open.spotify.com/album/2I10bEdFZUMfvMHi4lNIzF?si=uq122sU3S0C-K1_H10NdCg) - [[Foxtide]]

This album has quite the range for being just over half an hour long. Maybe not range but guardrails. At times, it's very [[indie rock]] with a pop twist (thinking [[flipturn]]) and other times it has the industrial, nearly electronic feel (like [[Muse]]). I liked it quite a lot even if the latter isn't usually my go to. Well worth the listen. 

→Check out 'Live by the Sun'
