[Autonomy](https://open.spotify.com/album/31NLkXt7yqS8Ugi7DvkxQm?si=_xEIz4D-RAarB4v8H_l3iA) - [[Stone]]

BRITROCK IS BACK, BABY. This Liverpool band’s second album is so infused with [[Oasis]] and what makes British rock music great. A seriously exciting effort that delighted and surprised throughout.

→Check out 'Rockmount'

[Prizefighter](https://open.spotify.com/album/4jnBkfheZqprSwXV7qyrc0?si=sPVmnehiSm22Q_wDirEIkg) - [[Mumford & Sons]]

I don't listen to M&S much, but I did quite like this album. While staying true to their sound, it feels like an updated and mature version of their sound that's brought in the latest of americana, folk, and country. Great use of featured artists, too. Totally worth the listen.

→Check out 'Begin Again'

[Goodbye (If You Call That Gone)](https://open.spotify.com/album/1svJQc2WYWqzXp75ZaxFH4?si=OICEaIRWSxicf0A8H7Dvhw) - [[MAY-A]]

Women are making the most interesting punk now and this is another example. It’s loud, funny and direct at times and then pop-driven and tender (ie Last Man On Earth). Her voice is also very adaptable and beautiful across the ranges of genres expressed in this. 11 songs, 34 minutes of great guitar music. Reminds me a bit of [[Poppy]]. 

→Check out '(I’m here for the) GIRLS'

[Triple Sabotage](https://open.spotify.com/album/6H9Y6nv2fMKTtyjkHhmEqT?si=dtWyXOzWSkyitgAuQqRxnQ) - [[Toys That Kill]]

Just a solid, veteran punk album but these legends. No-frills, sounds great and does exactly what it set out to do. 

→Check out 'Hero's Ashtray'

[Enema Of The Garden State](https://open.spotify.com/album/6eAJEJwTjiOQG5jvISsx1v?si=giBSHmZ0ThiM1fvrTdZEJA) - [[Madi Diaz]]

Technically out last week, but Madi Diaz released an acoustic covers album of [[Blink-182]] to raise money for anti-ICE activities. She really is a real one. 

→Check out 'Don't Leave Me'
