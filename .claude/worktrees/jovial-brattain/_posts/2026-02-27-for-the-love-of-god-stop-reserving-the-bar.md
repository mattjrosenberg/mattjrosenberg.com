> By reserving the bar you are alienating the immediate neighborhood because it makes it impossible for them to come in without a reservation. You are not a reliable option for the people who live nearby and don’t feel like cooking tonight. The people who, if they could, would be the ones keeping you in business because they are your immediate customers who don’t have to travel to get to you.

Just the loudest AMEN to this from Lula (who runs restaurants). Very few places can justify making the bar a managed space and it really kills the vibe. But, I resonated most with the quote above - I love the restaurants around me because I know I'm able to get in on a regular basis. We've let the desire for predictably ruin experiences and restaurants are better to become enticing to fill the bar.
