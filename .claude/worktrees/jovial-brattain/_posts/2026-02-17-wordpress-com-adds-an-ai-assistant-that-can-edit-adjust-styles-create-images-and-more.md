Automattic / WordPress is a company I tend to follow from a distance - partially because I worked for a competitor that did make a better product (for most people) and partially because they are so important to what the web actually is. But, they may be the least served by the direction the technology industry has taken over the last decade.

They're a bit too hard for lay-people to implement, resulting in many small operators paying for a single-price web dev for a site that then never changes. Then, they're also not advanced enough for some big options. This move - one they had to do before others did it for them - also probably just killed tons of small businesses bought into the open-source environment they built. I don't begrudge Automattic for doing it but I also get why the community they use to foster (and now fight) may be angry for this.

But, all that being said, this site was built over the weekend custom with an AI. It's tough to see that and think many of the website platforms aren't in danger. I see why the WYSIWYG builders are embracing AI more.
