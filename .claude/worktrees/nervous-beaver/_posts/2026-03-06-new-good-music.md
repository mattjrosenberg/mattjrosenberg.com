[Cash Rich](https://open.spotify.com/album/1kyWbqvt4hUrC6tHzuLMHL?si=8H620ZNDS3SU-KU8mEz41w) - [[Snake Eyes]]

Just incredibly solid and inviting indie rock. It's a bit noise and metal but mostly it is scrappy. Vocals and writing really elevate the solid rock basis this is built on top of. 

→Check out 'i'm a daydream'

[Werewolf](https://open.spotify.com/album/4LeD15SToHkoWTYGiVgFRQ?si=bNzg4a1aRSSx_UI4FYEm1g) - [[The Brook & The Bluff]]

I really just loved this. Such beautiful folk music that's peaceful but grabs you with deep roots in classic rock. Certainly will be getting a lot more plays soon.

→Check out 'Can't Figure It Out'

[Book of Churches](https://open.spotify.com/album/5U95ktQrWElN94jXjlbfDi?si=orqHtHQ_Sp6Z80xRyaE3eg) - [[Book of Churches]]

It is hard to hear this and not think of [[Bon Iver]]. It's such a soft and enveloping sound that mixes floating lyrics with light acoustic car. I really liked this album. 

→Check out 'There You Go I Love You'
